## Daily rules attached to the nine outcomes

### 1. Squat and overhead press

- First heavy session at least 48 hours after the return session: squat **185 lb for 3×5** and press
  **60 lb for 3×5**, if the warm-ups and first set remain technically sound and pain-free. If the
  return session occurred August 16, Monday is recovery/technique and Wednesday is the baseline.
- Monday and Friday are progress exposures. After a completed 3×5, the next heavy exposure adds
  **5 lb to squat** and **2.5 lb to press**. Microplates are required for the press.
- Wednesday uses about 80% of Monday's work-set load for 3×5, with controlled technique. It never
  becomes a hidden test day.
- First miss: repeat the same load at the next heavy exposure. Second miss at the same load: reduce
  7.5%, keep the same clock slot, and rebuild. The calendar never resets to zero.
- Warm-ups, pulls/hinges, upper-back work, and any coach-required accessories stay inside the
  75-minute session. No optional second workout.
- Daily: existing 5 g creatine protocol, protein at each meal, no unbounded “bulk.” The Sunday close
  uses a seven-day bodyweight average; food increases only when two weeks of recovery and training
  evidence justify it.
- The last new heavy exposure is April 30. May 1–5 is recovery/evidence reserve, not a panic max.

From 185 to 405 requires **44 successful 5 lb squat additions**. From 60 to 225 requires **66
successful 2.5 lb press additions**. There are approximately 74 Monday/Friday progress exposures in
the campaign: the squat has substantial repeat/deload reserve; the press has only about eight
exposures of reserve and is therefore the tighter physical critical path.
